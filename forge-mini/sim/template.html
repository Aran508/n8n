Arm design

All four arms are identical: a base yaw joint, three pitch joints and a tool servo. The tool always points straight down while working, which keeps the inverse kinematics simple and the gripper square to the plate.

Worst case: arm stretched out flat holding 30 g. Red tick = 50% of stall, the most a hobby servo should hold continuously. An MG90S on the shoulder would sit at 57%, which is why the shoulder and base use MG996Rs. Run forge budget to recompute after changing link lengths.

Software

How the code fits together

The AI never drives a motor. It writes a recipe, the planner proves the recipe safe in this simulation, and only then does the ESP32 move servos. The e-stop and watchdog sit underneath everything.

Claude / GPTWrites and fixes recipes through MCP tools: describe_cell, check_reach, plan_recipe, save_recipe, run_recipe (asks you first), stop.forge/mcp_server.py
Recipe plannerTurns steps like pick, place, hold, press and inspect into straight-line moves, checks reach and joint limits for every 20 mm waypoint, and rejects plans where more than two arms share the centre zone.forge/planner.py
